Fix `--version` output being suppressed by shell wrapper functions.

## What's Changed

### 🐛 Bug Fixes
- **Shell Wrapper `--version` Passthrough**: Updated shell wrapper scripts (`bash`, `zsh`, `fish`) generated by `cdx init` (and documented in `README.md`) to directly invoke `command cdx` when `--version` is passed as the first argument, preventing stdout capture from suppressing version output.
